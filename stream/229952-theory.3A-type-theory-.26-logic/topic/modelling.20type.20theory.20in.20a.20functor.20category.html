Stream: theory: type theory & logic

Topic: modelling type theory in a functor category

Till Rampe (Jul 17 2026 at 15:10):

I've made the observation that the functor category CIC^ICI models dependent type theory whenever the category CCC does so: Say CCC is a comprehension category with split fibration E:Cop→CatE : C^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathrm{Cat}E:Cop→Cat, then EI:(CI)op→CatE^I : ({C^I})^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathrm{Cat}EI:(CI)op→Cat, where EI(Γ)E^I (\Gamma)EI(Γ) is the category of sections of the split fibration

E∘Γop:Iop→Cat,E \circ \Gamma^{\mathrm{op}} : I^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathrm{Cat},E∘Γop:Iop→Cat,

defines a split fibration on CIC^ICI.

The first example that comes to my mind are presheaf categories; these inherit a lot of type theoretic structure from the category of sets: Σ\SigmaΣ-types, Π\PiΠ-types, universes etc.

Is there literature on lifting a model of type theory on CCC to a model on CIC^ICI? In particular, is there a uniform treatment of all type formers?

Mike Shulman (Jul 25 2026 at 19:11):

Some type formers are inherited by such a model, but Π\PiΠ-types and universes are not "inherited" from the base model in the sense of being pointwise. For Π\PiΠ-types you need some technology such as this, and for universes I don't know any technique that works for general CCC. But there is substantial literature on modeling type theory in presheaf categories; one keyword to look for is "Hofmann-Streicher universes".
