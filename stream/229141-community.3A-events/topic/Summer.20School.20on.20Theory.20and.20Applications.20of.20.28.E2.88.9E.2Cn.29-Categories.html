Topic: Summer School on Theory and Applications of (∞,n)-Categories

Nima Rasekh (Oct 05 2026 at 15:51):

We are pleased to announce the Summer School on Theory and Applications of (∞,n)-Categories.

The Summer School will take place from July 5 to 9, 2027, at BIRS-IAN on the UBC Okanagan campus in Kelowna, Canada.

It aims to introduce graduate students and early-career researchers to the theory of (∞,n)-categories and its applications in modern mathematics. The program will feature four coordinated lecture series by:

  • @Viktoriya Ozornova Viktoriya Ozornova (MPIM Bonn, Germany)
  • @Lorenzo Riva Lorenzo Riva (Harvard CMSA, USA)
  • Julia Plavnik (Indiana University, USA) — TBC
  • Theo Johnson-Freyd (Dalhousie University, Canada)

The first two lecture series will introduce participants to the theory of (∞,n)-categories. The other two will explore applications of higher categories to categorical representation theory and topological quantum field theory.

More information is available on the conference website (https://lynemoser.com/workshopncat/), and applications will open at a later date. In the meantime, save the week and stay tuned! We will share further information as soon as applications open!

Organizers:
Lyne Moser, @Nima Rasekh, @Martina Rovelli , and @Maru Sarazola
