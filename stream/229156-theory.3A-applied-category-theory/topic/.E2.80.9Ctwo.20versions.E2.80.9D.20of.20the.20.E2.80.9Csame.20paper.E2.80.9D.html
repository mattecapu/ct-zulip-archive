Ryan Wisnesky (Sep 23 2026 at 18:48):

It was not uncommon, at least a few decades ago, for CS conferences to have strict page limits but meet frequently, whereas journals had few page limits but only published infrequently. So it was not uncommon for a result to appear at a conference with the theorems stated without proof, and then an "extended version" to appear in a journal later, fleshing out the proofs and adding more content. The papers would have the same title and literally say "extended version", and the journals would even say, "must have 30% new content" or whatever. Sometimes the conferences would even say that there would be a follow up journal for extended editions.
