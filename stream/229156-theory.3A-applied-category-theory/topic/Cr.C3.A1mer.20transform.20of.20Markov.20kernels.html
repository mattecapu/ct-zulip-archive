Tobias Fritz (Aug 31 2026 at 06:30):

Those 2-cells are relative Rényi entropy at parameter ∞\infty∞. This is usually considered for probability measures, where D∞(P,Q)=inf⁡{κ≥0∣e−κP≤Q}D_\infty(P,Q) = \inf \{ \kappa \ge 0 \mid e^{-\kappa} P \le Q\}D∞​(P,Q)=inf{κ≥0∣e−κP≤Q}.
