Nathanael Arkor (Oct 21 2026 at 20:34):

To talk of left extensions of virtual double categories, you really need to work in a virtual double category: VDCs do not form a double category, merely a VDC. 2-categories are also insufficient, as the representably fully faithful 1-cells in the 2-category of VDCs are not the fully faithful functors of VDCs (see §2.4 here). So, the result I linked to should indeed be the most appropriate for your purposes.
