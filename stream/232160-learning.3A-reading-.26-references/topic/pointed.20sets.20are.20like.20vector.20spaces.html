Oscar Cunningham (Oct 30 2026 at 15:27):

I'm wondering if anyone has looked at the same question over ∞-groupoids. There I think the examples from Set break. Like a circle with a point on it isn't the disjoint union of some space with the point. And a chain complex which has dimension 0 at the bottom can't be the chain complex of any ∞-groupoid. So the analogues of pointed sets and vector spaces aren't always free.
