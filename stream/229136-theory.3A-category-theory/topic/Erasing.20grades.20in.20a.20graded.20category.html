Nathanael Arkor (Nov 03 2026 at 10:45):

Have these change of base functors been named before ?

I don't know where these constructions are explicitly mentioned anywhere, but they are most naturally seen from the perspective of graded categories as local discrete fibrations (over the delooping of G\mathbb GG). For this correspondence, see Theorem 9.6 and Corollary 10.9 of Exponentiable virtual double categories and presheaves for double categories, for instance.

Every G\mathbb GG-graded category induces a local discrete fibration over the delooping of G\mathbb GG, and thus is equipped with the structure of a bicategory (namely, the source of the local discrete fibration). When G\mathbb GG is strict, this bicategory is a 2-category, and its underlying category is the one you describe.

The third construction is given by taking the quotient of this 2-category by the relation induced by the 2-cells.
