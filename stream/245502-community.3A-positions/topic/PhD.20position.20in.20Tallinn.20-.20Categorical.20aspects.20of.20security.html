Topic: PhD position in Tallinn - Categorical aspects of security

Amar Hadzihasanovic (Nov 19 2026 at 09:38):

I am looking for a PhD student to work under my supervision on a project related to categorical aspects of composable security. This is part of a collaboration between TalTech and Cybernetica AS, funded by an Estonian TemTA grant.
The candidate should have an interest in applied category theory, formal methods for security, and/or programming language theory; a background in one or more of these areas is a bonus, but a strong foundation in mathematics and/or theoretical computer science will do.

Because of some expected changes in the university's PhD funding model, we are looking to hire someone quickly, ideally before February 2027.
The candidate needs to have a Master's degree. The expected PhD study time at TalTech is 4 years. A PhD is a salaried position and is topped with an additional tax-free stipend from the department, which ensures a good living standard in Estonia.

There is a vibrant, ever-growing community of category theorists, type theorists, and theoretical computer scientists at TalTech.
The PhD candidate would join the Compositionality group, which besides me, currently includes Pawel Sobocinski, Fosco Loregian, Mario Román, Elena Di Lavore, Alessandro Di Giorgio, Callum Reader, Vincent Moreau, Diana Kessler, Eigil Rischel, and a multitude of PhD students.
We also work closely together with the Logic and Semantics group, which includes category theorists and PL theorists such as Tarmo Uustalu, Niccolò Veltri, Nathanael Arkor, and Bryce Clarke.
The project coulds also contemplate collaboration with people at Cybernetica AS including Peeter Laud and Niels Voorneveld.

Tallinn is a beautiful city on the Baltic coast, just opposite Helsinki, which can be reached with a 2hr ferry ride. The TalTech campus is interspersed with forested areas, which cover a large part of the country, contributing to the 2nd best air quality in Europe after its neighbour Finland.
With generous paid parental leave (up to 18 months), a chart-topping education system, and marriage equality since 2024, Estonia is a great place for families. Candidates from all backgrounds are encouraged to apply.

Please write to me either here or via email (amar.hadzihasanovic@taltech.ee) if you want to discuss this further. You are also encouraged to contact former or current PhD students in our group about their experience.
