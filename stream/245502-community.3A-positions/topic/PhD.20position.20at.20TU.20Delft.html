PhD position at TU Delft: deriving formal languages from their semantics

I am looking for a PhD candidate for a 4-year position in the Department of Software Technology at TU Delft, funded by the Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR) and starting as soon as possible.

The project, "SemSyn: From Semantics to Syntax", studies how to start from a specification of a class of models and derive a formal language describing them. It involves:

  • dependent type theory and its categorical semantics (categories with families, comprehension categories);
  • generalized algebraic theories;
  • Makkai's signatures, given by inverse categories;
  • two-level type theory;
  • formalization in the Agda proof assistant, developed alongside the mathematics.

You should have a Master's degree in mathematics, computer science, or a related field, and experience in (or a strong motivation to learn) both category theory and theoretical computer science. Experience with a proof assistant is a plus, but not required.

Applications are evaluated on a rolling basis, so please apply early rather than waiting for the deadline of 29 November 2026. I very much encourage you to get in touch with me by email (B.P.Ahrens@tudelft.nl) before, and in addition to, submitting your official application.

Vacancy and application: https://careers.tudelft.nl/job/Delft-PhD-Position-Systematic-Derivation-of-Formal-Languages-and-Their-Correctness-2628-CD/1375074157/

Please forward this message to anyone who might be interested.
