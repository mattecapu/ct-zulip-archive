Call for Papers

10th Annual International Conference on Applied Category Theory (ACT2027)
28 June to 2 July 2027

Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA

https://zardini.mit.edu/act2027/

The 10th International Conference on Applied Category Theory will take place at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA, from 28 June to 2 July 2027. This conference follows previous events from Leiden (2018) to Tallinn (2026).

Applied category theory is important to a growing community of researchers who study computer science, logic, engineering, physics, biology, chemistry, social science, systems, linguistics and other subjects using category-theoretic tools. The background and experience of our members is as varied as the systems being studied. The goal of the Applied Category Theory conference series is to bring researchers together, strengthen the applied category theory community, disseminate the latest results, and facilitate further development of the field.

SUBMISSIONS

We accept submissions in English in the following three tracks:

  1. Research papers (to be published in proceedings, 12 pages + references)
  2. Research talks (not published, 2 pages + references)
  3. Software demonstrations (not published, 2 pages + references)

Details on how to prepare your submission will appear on the conference website.

Reviewing is single-blind. All submissions and (anonymous) reviews will be published on OpenReview after decisions are made.

AI POLICY (https://zardini.mit.edu/act2027/ai-policy/submissions/)

This policy is subject to discussion within the PC until early December 2026, so we recommend reviewing the website version by then. The current draft version follows here.

Authors who decided to use AI in the preparation of their submission should do so responsibly:

  1. Authors remain exclusively responsible for their submissions, in all their parts, and are expected to submit work of high quality, thoroughly checked, and geared towards the ACT audience in topic and style.

  2. All submissions must come with an AI disclosure statement, detailing which tasks (e.g. copywriting, editing, latex/tikz assistance, proof/definition ideation/checking, literature review, etc.) have been assisted by AI. If no AI was used in the preparation of the work, then this should also be disclosed.

Finally, we encourage authors to be mindful of the burden on reviewers that AI-generated content can impose.

IMPORTANT DATES

The following dates are all in 2027, and Anywhere On Earth.

  • Abstracts Due: 14 March
  • Full papers Due: 21 March
  • Authors notification: 3 May
  • Camera-ready version due: 31 May
  • Conference: 28 June -- 2 July
