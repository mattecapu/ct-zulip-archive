I am referring to a statement in Section 2.1 (p.8) of the paper "Enhanced 2-categories and limits for lax morphisms" by Stephen Lack and Michael Shulman. Given two monads TTT and SSS on a category, the statement says that the notion of a distributive law λ:TS⇒ST\lambda: TS\Rightarrow STλ:TS⇒ST is equivalent to giving a lifting of SSS to the Eilenberg-Moore category EM(T)EM(T)EM(T) of TTT. The relationship between these concepts is the subject of the paper Distributive laws by Beck, which explains how to go back and forth between them.
