Topic: parametrized multicategories

Jonas Frey (Aug 06 2026 at 10:19):

Let CCC be a multicategory. By a CCC-parametrized multicategory DDD I mean a structure which has a set/class D0D_0D0​ of morphisms, and multi-arrows which may have multiple inputs from CCC as well as DDD, and a unique output in DDD.

Has this notion been studied?

It would generalize [[multiactegories]] by allowing multiple DDD-inputs rather than a unique one for the morphisms.

Mike Shulman (Aug 15 2026 at 19:03):

Also worth noting that a pair consisting of C and a C-parametrized multicategory D is equivalent to a multicategory equipped with a functor to the multicategory freely generated by a monoid and an algebra over it.

Jonas Frey (Aug 15 2026 at 20:06):

Is C the domain of the functor? And what do you mean by algebra over a monoid ?

Mike Shulman (Aug 15 2026 at 20:21):

Let me just be explicit: by "the multicategory freely generated by a monoid and an algebra over it" I mean the multicategory with two objects c,dc,dc,d, one morphism (c,…,c)→c(c,\dots,c) \to c(c,…,c)→c of every arity (making ccc a monoid), and one morphism (c,…,c,d,…,d)→d(c,\dots,c,d,\dots,d) \to d(c,…,c,d,…,d)→d of every pair of arities. Then CCC is the fiber over ccc.

Jonas Frey (Aug 15 2026 at 20:28):

Thanks, that makes sense!
