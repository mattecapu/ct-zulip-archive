The old nForum used usernames, while the new one uses email. This worked for me:

1) Use "Forgot password"
2) Enter your email to reset your password, go to your mail box and reset your password (you can use your old one I guess)
3) When you login make sure you use your email address and not your username
4) Accept the admin acknowledgement (after logging in) and your are in
